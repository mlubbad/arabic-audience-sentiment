## Why
WO-9 (#61) fixed the Sync page timing out on AJ360. The cause was a per-page-view scan of about
195k comments, and the fix was to read `private.video_comment_stats`. WO-11 (#68) keeps that
table exact: triggers, a nightly reconcile, and a guard against moving comments.

Two more functions still count comments the slow way on a user action:

1. **`public.admin_channels()`** (latest definition in
   `supabase/migrations/20260919160000_admin_observability.sql`). It powers the admin
   **Channels** page and runs two correlated subqueries per channel:
   - `comment_count` = `count(*) from public.comments cm join public.videos v on v.id =
     cm.video_id where v.channel_id = c.id`
   - `video_count` = `count(*) from public.videos v where v.channel_id = c.id`

   Neither has a `workspace_id` predicate. The comment index is `comments_video_date
   (workspace_id, video_id, …)` and the video index is `videos_channel (workspace_id,
   channel_id)`, so neither subquery can use its index's leading column. Every channel
   therefore costs a scan, and the AJ360 channel alone has about 195k comments.
2. **`private.select_videos_impl`** (latest definition in
   `supabase/migrations/20261001010000_unlimited_plan_overflow_and_job_retries.sql:304`). It
   runs on every selection change on the Sync page's catalogue, and has two correlated
   `count(*) from public.comments` per video:
   - one for the estimate of the videos being added;
   - one for the `outstanding` sum across **every** selected video in the workspace.

   These use the index, but the second grows with the whole selection on each click.

## Notes
- `private.video_comment_stats.total` is exact by construction: triggers, plus the WO-11
  nightly reconcile. A video with no stats row means zero comments. That is why
  `coalesce(…, 0)` is correct and no fallback to `public.comments` is needed.
- `admin_workspace_footprint` and `admin_ops` also count comments. They are index-backed or
  admin-only one-offs, so they are out of scope. If you measure something different, say so
  in the PR instead of changing them.
