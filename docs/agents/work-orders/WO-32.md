## Why
Show a customer, per channel, which of the workspace's other channels share its engaged audience, how strongly, and through which videos. Show numbers about groups only, never people.

## Design (decided)
1. **Visibility.** The nav entry and the route render **only when `audience_overlap_available(ws)` returns `switch_on && plan_allows`**. Otherwise:
   - the nav shows nothing;
   - the route shows `NotFound`-style text ("This section isn't available"), never an upsell. The owner decided the section is not shown on the lower plans.
   - A signed-out reader sees the demo empty state, like `/competitors`.
   - While the switch is off in production, nobody sees the page.
2. **API module** `api/audience.ts`:
   - `loadAvailability(ws)`, `loadOverlap(channel)` and `loadSharedVideos(channel, other, limit)`;
   - zod schemas matching WO-30/31 exactly: every ratio `.nullable()`, `status` an enum;
   - errors mapped to the codes `forbidden | feature_unavailable | plan_feature_unavailable | invalid_input`;
   - query keys in an `audienceKeys` object;
   - **no Supabase call inside a component** (PLAYBOOK §5.2).
3. **The page** (`/audience?channel=`): a channel picker (`ui/Labelled` + `SelectItem`), like the competitors page. Then:
   - **Header:** the channel's engaged audience in 30 days ("our calculation"), `computed_at` formatted, and a line "Overlap is measured only between channels in this workspace, on the same platform".
   - **Overlap table** (`ui/Table` on wide screens, stacked cards under 640 px like `dashboard/Compare.tsx`). Columns:
     - channel;
     - their audience;
     - **share of your audience there** (the main number), with its Wilson interval as "12 % (9–15 %)";
     - share of theirs;
     - affinity, as a bar with a mark at 100.

     A badge **"High overlap"** shows when `share_of_mine > 0.30`.
   - **Below-threshold rows** show "Not enough data" in place of the numbers, **never 0 or "—0 %"**.
   - Every figure carries the **"our calculation"** label (it is a derived metric; policy).
4. **Venn card** for the selected row: a hand-drawn SVG using design tokens (no new library).
   - Circle areas follow |A| and |B| (approximately); the overlap is labelled with `shared` and the share.
   - It has a sentence explaining it, and an `aria-label` with the same numbers.
5. **Bridge videos** for the selected row (WO-31): title (linking to the platform's video URL in a new tab), published date, shared commenters, affinity.
   - `below_threshold` → the honest empty state;
   - an empty list → "No video reaches the privacy floor yet".
6. **Empty states:**
   - `pending`: "Measuring… first figures within 30 minutes";
   - no other channel on the platform: "Add a channel or competitor to measure overlap";
   - every row below threshold.
7. **Never show** a commenter name, id, hash or comment text anywhere on this page.

## Acceptance criteria (each test fails on a named guard mutation; say which line)
- [ ] **Visibility:**
  - `switch_on=false` or `plan_allows=false` → no nav entry, and the route shows "not available";
  - both true → the page;
  - signed out → the demo empty state.
- [ ] **Below threshold:** renders "Not enough data", and no "0" or "0 %" anywhere in that row.
- [ ] **Formatting:** a share of 0.1234 with CI 0.09–0.15 renders "12 % (9–15 %)" in en and with Arabic percent formatting in ar; affinity 240 shows the bar past the 100 mark.
- [ ] **"High overlap"** shows at 0.31 and not at 0.30.
- [ ] The **"our calculation"** label is on every derived figure, in both languages.
- [ ] Selecting a row loads bridge videos with `(channel, other, 10)`, and the Venn's `aria-label` has the pair's numbers.
- [ ] The empty states for `pending`, no other channels, and all rows below threshold.
- [ ] The zod schema test parses all-null ratios and `status: 'pending'`.
- [ ] `afterEach(cleanup)` is in every component test file; `ToastHost` sits outside the router (no `<Link>` in a toast).
- [ ] `npm run check` passes. **No e2e and no screenshots:** the PM does the visual check.
