1. What the application is

An Arabic-first audience-monitoring service for YouTube creators, marketing agencies and media companies in the Arabic-speaking market. A channel owner connects their own channel through Google OAuth; the service catalogues the channel's uploads, reads comments and replies on the videos the owner selects, classifies each comment's sentiment with an Arabic-dialect model run on our own server and its topic, and shows the owner trends over time, comparisons between videos, and the underlying comments. It is read-only: no write endpoint of the YouTube Data API is called, and no channel is read except through its owner's authorization.

Independent value to the YouTube ecosystem: Arabic is the largest language without a working comment-sentiment tool; existing tools are English-first and mis-classify dialectal Arabic. Creators and agencies in the region currently read thousands of comments by hand or not at all. The service gives them a measured, honestly-labelled view of how their audience reacts — with YouTube's data shown as YouTube's, and our analysis shown as ours.

Scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. Reading commentThreads.list on the owner's own videos with youtube.readonly alone is refused with insufficientPermissions; Google offers no narrower scope that permits reading comments as the owner, so force-ssl is requested although the application performs no write. This is documented in the application's source (supabase/functions/oauth-start/index.ts).

4. Expected traffic and why 10,000 units/day is insufficient

LoadUnits
Connect + discover a large channel (≈4,500 videos)≈ 180 once
First read of a large channel (≈170,000 comments)≈ 1,700 once
Daily scheduled checks, one creator (20 selected videos, daily)1 + new comments
Daily scheduled checks, one agency (10 channels × 20 videos × 4 checks)≈ 16 + new comments
Steady state at 100 subscribers≈ 600 – 1,500 / day
Peak day: five large onboardings + steady state≈ 12,000

The quota is per Google Cloud project, not per user. A single day with two or three large-channel onboardings reaches 10,000 units and blocks every other customer's scheduled read until midnight Pacific. Requested: 50,000 units/day — headroom for onboarding days at 300 subscribers without failing anyone's scheduled reads. Change detection (§3) keeps steady-state usage low regardless of the number of channels.

  • Each workspace's rows are isolated by Postgres row-level security; tenants cannot read each other, verified by tests against direct requests.
  • No data is sold, shared with other users, or aggregated across channels.
  • Revocation: the user can disconnect the channel in the application (two-step confirmation), or revoke at myaccount.google.com/permissions; an invalid_grant answer marks the connection revoked and cancels pending work immediately.
  • Deletion: on request by e-mail; completed within 7 days.
  • The privacy policy (/privacy-policy) and terms (/terms) link the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy and state the above.

6. Compliance statements

  • The application name does not contain "YouTube".
  • YouTube data is displayed alongside our own analysis, each clearly labelled; the service does not replace YouTube's own metrics.
  • Authorized data is not stored beyond 30 days without being refreshed (comment text purged; metadata refreshed each check).
  • No write, rating, commenting or moderation action is performed through the API.
  • A user may withdraw access at any time; the withdrawal takes effect at the next scheduled check.
