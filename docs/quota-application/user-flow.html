Audience Sentiment — user flow and where each YouTube API call happens

Units are YouTube Data API quota units. A channel is read only after its owner's consent, and only the videos the owner selected.

1

Sign up · workspace

Email sign-in. A personal workspace is created; an agency adds one per client. Free plan by default.

no YouTube call

2

Connect own channel

Google consent screen (youtube.readonly, youtube.force-ssl). The token's own channel becomes the workspace's channel.

channels.list(mine=true) · 1 unit

3

Catalogue

The channel's uploads are listed with their reported comment counts so the owner can choose.

playlistItems.list · 1 / 50 videos
videos.list · 1 / 50 videos

4

Select videos vs plan

The owner picks videos; the server refuses a selection beyond the plan's comment allowance. Nothing is read yet.

no YouTube call

5

Scheduled read

Per plan interval: counts first, then comments only for videos whose count moved.

videos.list(statistics) · 1 / 50
commentThreads.list · 1 / 100 comments
comments.list · replies

6

Dashboard

Sentiment and topic trends, video comparison, the comments themselves with our labels, CSV export. YouTube data is shown as YouTube's, our analysis as ours.

no YouTube call

↻ Every interval

Creator: daily · Agency: every 6 h · Media: every 2 h. Unchanged videos are deferred without a comment read. Measured live on a 789-video channel: second pass over 100 videos = 2 units, 0 reads.

Leaving

Disconnect channel (two presses): the grant is revoked, scheduled work cancelled, nothing else read. Retire workspace: the owner's access ends and the workspace passes to the operator. Revoke at Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions): the next call fails with invalid_grant and the channel is marked for reconnection immediately. Deletion of personal data: on request by email, within 7 days.

Data lifecycle

Comment text is purged 30 days after collection by a daily job; ids, dates and counts are refreshed at every check; our own labels are kept for the owner's history. Each workspace's rows are isolated by row-level security. No data is sold, shared with other users, or aggregated across channels.

Why the default 10,000 units/day is not enough: the quota is per Google project, not per user. Onboarding one large Arabic channel (≈4,500 videos, ≈170,000 comments) costs ≈1,900 units once; two or three onboardings in a day reach the ceiling and every other customer's scheduled read fails until midnight Pacific. Steady state at 100 subscribers is ≈600–1,500 units/day. Requested: 50,000 units/day.
